# Group Writing Style Guide

Voice reference for the trip website copy and dummy data, built from the "Boys in Miamiii call me zestyyy" group chat (Jun 29 to Jul 3, 2026, 232 messages). Use this when writing ANY user-facing text for the site so it sounds like the group instead of a template.

> Heads up: this file contains real quotes from the chat. Fine for local use and the site's copy inspiration, but don't ship the file itself in a public bundle or commit it to a public repo.

## The vibe in one paragraph

Six ATL friends in their mid-20s planning a "chill unc trip", not a rager: private pool, grill, beach walkable, casino nearby. Lowercase, fast, short messages fired in bursts. Hype is loud (caps, stretched letters), logistics are casual but sharp (exact prices, flight times), and every decision gets roasted on the way to getting made. Affectionate insults, inside jokes, tapbacks as votes. Nobody writes paragraphs. Nobody punctuates like it's an email.

## Mechanics (how they physically type)

- **Short bursts over paragraphs.** Three quick messages beat one long one: "LMaoo fuck now I can't choose" then "I WANT THIS SHIT TOO" then "South FL penthouse goes hard" (Lucas, 90 seconds).
- **Stretched letters for emphasis**: Miamiii, zestyyy, YOOOOO, Wooooow, LOOOL, LOLOLL, Shiiiiiii, siccc, WTFFF, Gahhdam, Ooooo, Awwwwww yeahhhhh.
- **Caps = hype or outrage**, used selectively: "I WANT THIS SHIT TOO", "HOW MUCH?!?", "we HAVE to buy tonight", "I'm DOWN!"
- **Loose punctuation.** Questions often skip the question mark ("Will there be a casino", "Isn't tom coming at 10 too"). Apostrophes optional ("Im locked in"). Periods rarely end a message; when they do it's for effect ("What. An. Upset.").
- **Emoji are sparse and pointed**, not decorative: 😂 doubled for real laughs, 🧐 for suspicion ("what's the catch here 🧐"), the occasional :( and 🏊 from Sia. Reactions carry the emoji load instead.
- **Tapbacks are first-class responses**: Loved, Liked, Laughed, Emphasized, Questioned, plus custom ones (🖕🏾 when Pete suggested Lucas share a bed). A tapback IS a reply and often a vote.
- **Numbers stay inline and exact**: "max $100 per night per person ($1600 total)", "$1576 + $30/day resort fees + $28/day valet = $1808", "$208? I think?"

## How decisions read in this group

Someone posts a link with a one-line pitch, others drop short verdicts ("Yo this is nice. I like", "Looks siccc"), tapbacks count as votes, and the closer is always a soft ultimatum: "Aight ima book this place tonight when I get home unless someone is opposed." Objections are blunt and immediate ("Nah wtf too much"). Use this shape for any voting/RSVP UI copy.

## Writing for the website

**The formula**: short + lowercase-leaning + confident hype + light roast + inside reference where it earns its place. If a line reads like a travel agency wrote it, delete it.

Don't (anti-patterns): "Explore our curated selection of premium accommodations!", "We're thrilled to announce...", long paragraphs, hashtag energy, emoji confetti, perfect punctuation everywhere, explaining the jokes.

## The PG dial

The real chat runs PG-13 (shit, fk, mfer, wtf, one 🖕🏾). The site is meant to be "very PG and cutie patootie" per Sia, then tuned toward the real vibe. Two registers:

- **Authentic (private use)**: keep the profanity exactly as attested. fk/fked stays abbreviated like the chat, "mfer" over the full word, 🖕🏾 allowed as a reaction.
- **PG-public**: swap "mfers" for "the boys", drop f-bombs entirely or use "fk" at most, keep "shit" out and let caps + stretched letters carry the energy ("this GOES HARD", "WTFFF" reads fine without spelling anything out). Keep the roasts; they're the personality. "Joe's church" is PG. "Harrahs style" is PG. The vibe survives sanitization if the rhythm and inside jokes stay.

When Sia says "make it sound like us", default to PG-public for anything deployed, authentic for anything private, and ask only if it's ambiguous which one a surface is.
